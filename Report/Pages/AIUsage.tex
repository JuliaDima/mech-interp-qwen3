used as coding assistants throughout the project.

and adherence to good software engineering practice: 
docstrings, type annotations, repetitive utility functions, 
and minor debugging. The overall experiment design, 
the conceptual structure of the analysis pipeline, 
verification of results, and the substantive implementation 
choices are the author's own.
