\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This thesis has investigated whether mechanistic structures described in Anthropic's \textit{On the Biology of a Large Language Model} transfer to \texttt{Qwen3-4B-Instruct}, an independently trained open-access model, and has extended that investigation through concept localisation and targeted knowledge editing.

The reproducibility study confirmed that carry-related lookup table features analogous to those in Claude 3.5 Haiku are present in Qwen3-4B, with a comparable positional structure: operand token positions encode individual digit values independently, while the \texttt{=} token encodes their joint modular combination. Attribution graphs over the linearised transcoder model recover the same high-level circuit topology, and the multilingual experiment demonstrates that operation, operand, and output-language components can be independently intervened on, suggesting that the mechanistic organisation identified by Lindsey et al.\ is not specific to a single model family.

The concept-localisation study reveals that concepts differ sharply in their geometric structure. Carry propagation produces a clean, localised binary direction at identifiable anchor positions and layers. GCD divisibility is similarly discriminatively encoded, with multiple anchor positions exceeding the null permutation band. Residue class membership, by contrast, yields no anchor position that consistently exceeds the null band, a result consistent with a Fourier-structured circular embedding in which information is distributed across all residues simultaneously rather than concentrated in a single binary direction.

The soft prompting experiments show that this geometric distinction has downstream consequences for knowledge editing. Where carry and GCD exhibit sharp concept directions, soft prompts produce limited benefit; where the concept resists localisation, as in balanced parentheses (+28.6 percentage points) and causal direction (+53.8 percentage points), learned prefix vectors can substantially shift model behaviour without modifying base parameters. The null result on residue class (+1\%) is consistent with the absence of a clean representational target for the prefix to exploit.

The results in this thesis suggest that mechanistic structure identified in one model family transfers to independently trained instruction-tuned models, that the geometry of concept representations varies systematically with the algebraic structure of the task, and that soft interventions are most effective precisely where circuit-level localisation fails. Future work should examine whether the Fourier structure hypothesised for residue class can be confirmed directly through feature-level analysis, and whether the soft prompting gains on causal direction generalise across prompt templates and model scales.
